# Code of conduct

Skyline has one maintainer. What follows is not a policy borrowed from a
foundation with a moderation team — it is what that one person will
actually do.

## What is expected

Be decent to people. Assume whoever you are replying to is trying to build
something and has less context than you do, rather than that they are
stupid or arguing in bad faith.

Disagreement is fine and useful. Arguing about the work is what a code
review is for. Going after the person instead of the work is not.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, in public or in private, after being asked to stop
- Insults or demeaning remarks aimed at a person rather than their work
- Bringing up someone's race, gender, sexuality, disability, religion,
  nationality or age as an argument or an insult
- Sexual attention or imagery, anywhere in the project
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Threats of violence, or encouraging anyone toward self-harm

This list is not exhaustive, and hunting for the gap between two items on
it is itself the behaviour being described.

## Where it applies

Issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere else this project is
discussed. It also applies when you are speaking for Skyline somewhere
else.

## Reporting

Email <skyline@hellodavid.ca>. It reaches the maintainer and nobody else.

Say what happened and where. Screenshots or links help. You do not have to
have a position on what should be done about it.

## What happens then

One person reads these, in their own time, so a reply comes when the
maintainer sees it. There is no rotation and no committee.

What follows depends on what happened. Roughly, in order: a private word, a
public correction, a comment or commit removed, a block from the
repository. Serious enough conduct skips the earlier steps.

There is no appeals process, because an appeals process needs a second
person. If the one you need to report is the maintainer, that is what
[GitHub's abuse reporting](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) is for,
and using it is a reasonable thing to do rather than a last resort.

## When this stops being enough

This is sized for one maintainer and no outside contributors. If that
changes, it needs rewriting by more than one person, and probably needs a
recognised standard such as the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) behind it.
Until then, a borrowed document describing moderators who interview
witnesses would be describing people who do not exist.
